# City Care Clinic (demo)

## Opening hours
The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 am to 1:00 pm. It is closed on Sunday.

## Consultation fees
General physician: Rs 1,500. Dermatology (skin specialist): Rs 2,500. Pediatrics (child specialist): Rs 2,000.
A follow-up visit within 7 days costs half the fee.

## Payment
Pay at the reception on the day of your visit, by cash or card.

## Cancellation policy
Please cancel at least 2 hours before your appointment by calling the reception.

## What to bring
Bring your ID card, old prescriptions and any recent test reports.

## Location
12 Main Boulevard, City Centre (demo address). Free parking is available.

## Emergencies
The clinic does not handle emergencies. Call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.

## Cancellation and changes
You can change or cancel an appointment  up to 2 hours before it starts
